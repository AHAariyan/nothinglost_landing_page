FAQs

Questions, answered.

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that shows the message text in its notifications: chat apps, social apps and SMS. You choose which apps to keep.

Can it recover messages deleted before I installed it?

No. NothingLost can only save messages that arrive as a notification after you install it.

Why was a deleted message not saved?

NothingLost saves what the notification shows. If a chat is muted, or was open on your screen when the message came in, the chat app may not show a notification, so there is nothing to save. Photos, voice notes and videos aren't saved, only text.

Will the other person know?

No. NothingLost only reads notifications on your own phone. Reading a message in NothingLost doesn't mark it as read in the chat.

Does it drain my battery?

No. It saves a few lines of text when a notification arrives and does nothing in between.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the Play Store: tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel NothingLost Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.
