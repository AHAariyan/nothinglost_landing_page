for your phone.

Many phones close apps in the background to save battery. Pick your brand to see what to switch on. Recover's checklist takes you to most of these screens.

Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO MIUI and HyperOS

  1. Notification accessYour phone shows a red "Danger" page for every app that asks. Tick the box at the bottom, wait for the timer, tap OK, then press Back to return to Recover.
  2. Battery saver: No restrictionsRecover opens its own battery page. Choose No restrictions.
  3. AutostartThe next screen lists your apps. Find Recover and switch it on.
  4. Lock it in recent apps"Clear all" closes Recover otherwise. Open recent apps, long-press or pull down the Recover card, and tap the lock.
  5. Keep WhatsApp running tooThese phones can close WhatsApp itself, and then no message arrives to save. Allow its Autostart and set its battery saver to No restrictions.

FAQs

Questions,
answered.

Something else? Write to us.

Can it recover messages deleted before I installed it?

No. Recover can only keep messages that arrive as a notification after you set it up. Nothing from before can be brought back.

I installed it. Why is the Deleted tab empty?

It fills up the first time someone deletes a message they sent you, and that can take a while. To see it work right away, tap Try it now on the Deleted tab: Recover sends itself a test message, deletes it, and shows you the copy it kept.

Which apps does it work with?

It recognises deleted messages in WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business and unsent messages in Messenger. It also keeps a copy of messages from any other app that shows the text in its notifications, such as Telegram, Instagram, Signal, Viber and SMS. You choose which apps to save.

Does it save photos, videos or voice notes?

No, only text. Recover saves what the notification shows, so for a photo or a voice note you see the line the chat app put in the notification, not the file itself.

Why was a deleted message not saved?

Recover saves what reaches your phone as a notification. If a chat is muted, or was open on your screen when the message came in, the chat app may not show a notification, so there is nothing to save. If your phone closed Recover in the background, messages in that time are missed: Recover tells you, and the checklist shows how to stop it happening.

Does it drain my battery?

No. It saves a few lines of text when a notification arrives and does nothing in between. On phones that close background apps it keeps a small Protection notification so it isn't stopped; you can hide that notification in Settings.

My Xiaomi phone shows a red "Danger" page. Is that safe?

It's the page Xiaomi, Redmi and POCO phones show for every app that asks for notification access. Tick the box at the bottom, wait for the timer, tap OK, then press Back to return to Recover. Your messages still never leave your phone.

Will the other person know?

No. Recover only reads notifications on your own phone. Reading a message in Recover doesn't mark it as read in the chat.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Everything stays on your phone.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the Play Store: tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel Recover Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
