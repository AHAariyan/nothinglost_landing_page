Good to know before you install

We'd rather tell you now than have you find out later.

  • It can't bring back old messages. It only saves messages that arrive after you install it.
  • It needs a notification. If a chat is muted, or open on your screen when the message comes in, the chat app may not show a notification, so there's nothing to save.
  • Text only. Photos, voice notes and videos aren't saved, only the text shown in the notification.

Questions

Which apps does it work with?

Any app that shows the message text in its notifications: chat apps, social apps and SMS. You choose which apps to keep.

Will the other person know?

No. NothingLost only reads notifications on your own phone. It doesn't open the chat or send anything, so nothing changes on their side. Reading a message in NothingLost doesn't mark it as read in the chat.

Does it drain my battery?

No. It saves a few lines of text when a notification arrives and does nothing in between. A small silent notification keeps it running, and you can hide it.

Where are my messages stored?

Only on your phone. They're never uploaded. Uninstalling the app, or "Delete all" in Settings, removes them.

How do I cancel Pro?

In the Play Store: tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions, and cancel NothingLost Pro. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.
